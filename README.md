# 🌾 AURA – Autonomous Unified Rural Agriculture

> **AI-driven autonomous systems to modernize and unify rural farming operations.**

AURA (Autonomous Unified Rural Agriculture) is an intelligent agriculture platform designed to empower farmers through automation, real-time monitoring, and data-driven decision-making. By integrating IoT, AI, cloud connectivity, and embedded systems, AURA aims to increase productivity, reduce resource wastage, and promote sustainable farming practices.

This project was **pitched at Idea Tribe 2025**, organized by **Jharkhand University of Technology (JUT), JUT-CIIE, Jharkhand Entrepreneur Association, and TexMin**, where I served as the **Team Leader of Team Green Bites**.

---

# 🚀 Vision

To build a unified smart agriculture ecosystem that enables farmers to monitor, automate, and optimize farming operations using affordable embedded systems and artificial intelligence.
